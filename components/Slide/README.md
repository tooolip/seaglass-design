A 16:9 slide frame for decks: a shore background, solid panels, bold rounded titles and pebbles as accents. Classes: `.slide` (add `.shore`), `.slide-inner`, `.slide-eyebrow`, `.slide-title`, `.slide-text`, `.slide-panel`, `.slide-footer`. Sizes use container units, so a slide scales with its width.

**You provide:** one `section.slide` per slide with an `aria-label`; one title; the deck title and slide number in the footer.

**Use it for:** decks, talk slides and social images.

**Do**
- One idea and one title per slide.
- Use two or three pebbles as accents on title slides, not on content slides.

**Don't**
- Use frosted `.glass` panels on slides; they do not export well.
