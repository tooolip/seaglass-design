Rows grouped inside one frosted card, divided by hairlines, with sea-glass icon tiles. Classes: `.glass.list`, `.list-row`, `.list-icon` (add `.pebble` and a hue class), `.list-text`, `.list-title`, `.list-sub`, `.list-meta`.

**You provide:** a `ul` of `li` items, each an `a.list-row` (opens something) or `div.list-row` (shows a value); a trailing chevron on rows that open a page.

**Use it for:** settings, menus, short lists of projects or documents.

**Do**
- Give each section of an app its own hue and reuse it on its icon tile.
- Keep one line of title and at most one line of subtitle.

**Don't**
- Put more than about 12 rows in one card; split them into titled groups.
